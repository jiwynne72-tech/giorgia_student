Present and Past Habits

• Present Simple / Past Simple: Repeated actions or states.
• Used to + infinitive: Past states or regular past habits that no longer happen.
• Would + infinitive: Past actions or behavior typical for someone (not states).
• Present/Past Continuous with 'always': Complain about annoying persistent behavior.

Verb Patterns & Combinations

• Verb + to + infinitive (agree, decide, manage, refuse, etc.)
• Verb + object + to + infinitive (advise, encourage, force, remind, etc.)
• Verb + -ing (admit, avoid, enjoy, keep, spend time, etc.)
• Verb + base form (let, make)

1. I decided ___ school and find a job.

2. My sister agreed ___ me with my homework.

3. Robert's mother encouraged him ___ Law.

5. Do his parents let him ___ with his friends?

Verbs with Change in Meaning

• Stop + -ing = finish an action. | Stop + to-infinitive = pause in order to do something.
• Remember + -ing = past memory. | Remember + to-infinitive = duty/reminder not to forget.
• Try + -ing = experiment. | Try + to-infinitive = make an effort.

1. Liz stopped ___ coffee. (She doesn't drink coffee anymore.)

2. Liz stopped ___ some coffee. (She paused in order to drink coffee.)

3. Jenny remembers ___ the door. (She has a memory of locking it.)

4. Jenny remembers ___ the door. (Duty: Don't forget to do it!)

5. He tried ___ the computer off as an experiment to fix the glitch.

6. He tried ___ the heavy computer on, but it was completely broken.

7. I remember ___ him last summer when we were in Rome.
