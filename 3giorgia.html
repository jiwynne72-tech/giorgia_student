Focus: Adjectives vs. Adverbs, Quantifiers & Narrative Tenses

1. Adjectives vs. Adverbs (Good vs. Well)

Adjectives (Good) describe nouns (people, things, states): She is a good singer.
Adverbs (Well) describe verbs (how an action is performed): She sings well.
Note: "Well" can also mean healthy ("I don't feel well today").

2. Quantifiers: Much, All of, & A lot of

Much: Used primarily with uncountable nouns in negative statements and questions (not much homework).
A lot of: Used in positive statements for large quantities with both countable and uncountable nouns.
All of: Refers to 100% of a specified group or set (all of the questions).

2. She spent hours studying for the exam.

4. There wasn't interest in the topic at first.
5. They ate pizza at the party yesterday.
6. I've finished my homework for tomorrow.
